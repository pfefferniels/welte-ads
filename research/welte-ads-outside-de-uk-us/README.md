# Welte-Mignon ads outside Germany, the UK and the USA

These are transcriptions of 90 print advertisements for the Welte-Mignon, from 1907–1930, published outside Germany, the UK and the USA. They are research material for the collection and are not yet encoded in TEI.

Each ad was found in a digitised newspaper or magazine and checked against the collection's criteria:

- a print ad from 1905–1930;
- for the Welte-Mignon;
- placed by Welte, a licensee or a dealer.

Two further rules apply here:

- **No "Welte among many".** Ads where the Welte-Mignon is one item in a list, appears only in the firm line, or is secondary to another product were excluded.
- **No paid notices.** Advertorials, "échos", reading notices and other text set like news copy were excluded.

Every ad was transcribed from the page scan, not from OCR. The rules are in [conventions.md](conventions.md). Each ad has one transcription, from one printing; its other printings are listed under it. Each regional file ends with the items excluded on re-inspection and the reasons.

**Borderline** ads are marked in the index below; drop them if a strict reading is wanted. The other notes mean:

- "two products": the other product is left out as `[gap: …]`, as the edition does;
- "demonstration": an ad announcing a Welte-Mignon recital or demonstration;
- "used" / "single instrument": a dealer ad for one instrument.

## Gaps

These archives could not be searched, so these countries are unsearched, not empty:

- **Blocked by bot checks or CAPTCHAs:**
  - Australia (Trove) and New Zealand (Papers Past);
  - Sweden (tidningar.kb.se), Iceland (timarit.is);
  - Spain: BNE Hemeroteca, BVPH, ABC, La Vanguardia;
  - Singapore (NewspaperSG) and Hong Kong (MMIS).
- **Austria (ANNO):** none of its 162 hits was a verifiable ad, and it then blocked access. A lead: the Klavierfirma Bernhard Kohn, Vienna, held the Welte agency (*Die Zeit*, 4 Jan 1914).
- **Belgium (BelgicaPress):** pages after 1918 can't be viewed online. A lead: P. Anthonis, Antwerp, Welte-Mignon agent (1912).
- **Denmark:** Mediestream pages from 1927 onwards are restricted.
- **Mexico:** *El Porvenir* (Monterrey), about 120 "welte" hits from 1926–1930, can only be viewed on site at the library.
- **Italy:** a lead, cav. Fornaciari, Rome, "rappresentante del Welte-Mignon-Piano" (1909).
- **Gallica:** blocked. FR-01 was transcribed from the RetroNews page image instead.
