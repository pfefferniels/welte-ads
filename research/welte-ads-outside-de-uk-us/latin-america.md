# Welte-Mignon advertisements — Latin America (Mexico, Cuba, Brazil, Chile, Argentina)

Diplomatic transcriptions from the scan images (not from OCR). No qualifying ads were found for
Spain, Portugal, Italy, Greece, Turkey or Russia (see [Gaps](README.md#gaps)).

### MX-01 — THE WELTE Artistic Player Piano
- **Periodical:** The Mexican Herald, Mexico City, Sunday 19 May 1907, p. 7
- **Advertiser:** A. Wagner & Levien; Sucs., Zuleta 13 y 14, Mexico ("General Agents for the Entire Mexican Republic")
- **Language:** English
- **Transcribed from:** https://hndm.iib.unam.mx/consulta/publicacion/visualizar/558075be7d1e63c9fea1a34d?pagina=558a34567d1ed64f16a3f6cc (PDF: https://hndm.iib.unam.mx/consulta/publicacion/pdf/558a34567d1ed64f16a3f6cc.pdf)
- **Further printings:** none found in the HNDM full-text search.
- **Notes:** The word "Mignon" does not appear. "Welte Artistic Player Piano" was the name the Welte-Mignon was sold under in America at the time (M. Welte & Sons / Welte Artistic Player Piano Co.), so it counts as a Welte-Mignon ad. The date and page come from the page header. Two places to watch: the T of "The" in the first text line is broken in the print, and the mark after "LEVIEN" looks like a semicolon (it could be a comma with a speck).

```text
THE

WELTE

Artistic Player Piano

The Welte gives the abso-
lutely true reproduction of
the interpretation of the
World's Most Famous
Pianists.

General Agents for the Entire Mexican Republic:
A. WAGNER & LEVIEN; SUCS.
Zuleta 13 y 14.        MEXICO.
```
Typography: Framed with an ornamental rope border. All lines centred. "THE" is in decorated capitals with three small ornaments on each side. "WELTE" is in very large decorated capitals and "Artistic Player Piano" in large type. The body text is set in a widely spaced display face, with "World's Most Famous" letter-spaced. "General Agents …" is set in condensed type. The firm line is in large capitals.
