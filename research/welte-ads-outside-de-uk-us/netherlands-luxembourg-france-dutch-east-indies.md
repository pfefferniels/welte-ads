# Welte-Mignon advertisements — Netherlands, Luxembourg, France, Dutch East Indies (1905–1930)

Transcribed from the page images: Delpher JP2 page scans at full resolution, eluxemburgensia IIIF at full resolution, and RetroNews page images at the highest zoom. No OCR was used for the text. Selection and transcription follow [conventions.md](conventions.md).

Notes on method:
- Delpher links go through the KB resolver, which redirects to the Delpher viewer. Page numbers come from the scan sequence.
- The small numbers printed at the edge of many ads (e.g. "Recl. 45333 40", "33113.50") are the newspaper's own advertisement reference numbers. They are transcribed where they fall inside the ad's space.
- Dutch quotation marks are given as printed: „…” (low opening, high closing).
- Belgium: no ad qualified. See "Excluded on re-inspection".

---

- **Further printings:** Bataviaasch Nieuwsblad, 10 Oct 1911, p. 11 (ddd:011035900:mpeg21:a0051). OCR only, same wording.
- **Notes:** Same announcement as ID-01, but reset and reworded ("gebracht en nog niet op Java geimporteerd is"; "Administrateur"), so it is a separate design. "geimporteerd" is printed without a diaeresis.

```text
Heden ontpakt:
EEN REPRODUCTIONS PIANO
Welte Mignon.
Dit is het fraaiste, beste en nieuwste instrument,
dat tot heden op dat gebied in den handel
gebracht en nog niet op Java geimporteerd is.
Het eerste exemplaar door mij nu verkocht aan
een Administrateur in de Preanger wordt tot en
met 15 October voor belangstellenden tentoon-
gesteld bij den alleenvertegenwoordiger.

Edmund Salzmann,
Piano- en Muziekhandel,
NOORDWIJK No. 12, WELTEVREDEN.
Attesten liggen ter inzage.                          8986
```
Typography: "Heden ontpakt:" is in bold italic. The second line is in condensed caps. "Welte Mignon." is in very large heavy condensed type. The text is in bold sans. "Edmund Salzmann," is in large bold Clarendon-style roman. "Piano- en Muziekhandel," is in small bold italic. The address is in bold sans caps. The last line is in large bold roman.

## Excluded on re-inspection

- **Kettner, "Een expositie van Kettner occasions", 1–2 Nov 1930** (Arnhemsche Courant, Haagsche Courant, Algemeen Handelsblad, De Standaard, De Tijd, Gooi- en Eemlander, Utrechtsch Prov. en Sted. Dagblad): "Welte among many". It is a sale list of about 40 used instruments, with the Tormin-Welte and Feurich Welte Quadrola as two lines.
- **A. G. J. Logjes, Arnhem, 1919–1923** (Arnhemsche Courant, all printings): "Welte among many". These are general piano-shop ads and brand lists. In the 1921 ad the Steinway Welte Mignon is one closing sentence.
- **Gebrs. Rijken & De Lange, De Maasbode 10 Nov 1922 ("Uitnoodiging … Afternoon-tea")**: "Welte among many" / other subject. It is a 70th-anniversary invitation with the Violina, the Steinway-Welte, a Phonola piano and a dance performance.
- **C. A. Muus, NRC 26 Apr 1925 and Rotterdamsch Nieuwsblad 27 Apr 1925** (overrules the preliminary "keep"): "Welte among many". It is a general Muus piano-shop ad with seven bulleted lines (new pianos from fl. 425, used instruments, hire purchase, main agency for "The Auto Piano", Welte-Mignon (Licensee), demonstrations, roll depot).
- **The Auto Piano Company, opening of its Rotterdam branch, 14 Nov 1924** (De Maasbode, De Telegraaf, NRC, Rotterdamsch Nieuwsblad) (overrules "check"): the Welte-Mignon is secondary to another subject. The ad is about the opening of the branch at Eendrachtsweg 13, and the Welte-Mignon Auto Piano is named once, alongside The Original Auto Piano, among the instruments demonstrated.
- **Mossel & Versteege, Haagsche Courant 13 Mar 1926**: Welte secondary. "Piano’s en Vleugels" and "De origineele Auto-Piano" dominate, and "Welte Mignon Reproductie-Piano’s" is the last of three items.
- **Pianohaus Hilger, Advertentieblad voor Limburg 12 Nov 1927** (overrules "keep"): "Welte among many". It is a brand list (Welte-Mignon, Steinway & Sons, Blüthner, Hilger, Gerbstädt, cheap pianos, Kastner Autopiano).
- **Pianohaus Eduard Hilger, Aachen, Limburger Koerier 3, 4 and 6 Dec 1930** (overrules "keep"): "Welte among many". It is a "Vorteilhaftes Angebot" sale list of eight instruments; the "Steinway-Flügel mit eingebautem Welte-Mignon" is one line.
- **W. Wolthers, Nieuwsblad van het Noorden 25 Sep, 9 Oct and 16 Oct 1922**: "Welte among many". It is a general piano-shop ad (Ibach, Steinweg, pianolas) with one Welte-Mignon paragraph.
- **W. Wolthers, De Noord-Ooster 17 Aug 1922**: "Welte among many". It is an exhibition notice listing the Welte-Mignon, an Ibach grand, pianos, organs and the Sonora talking machine.
- **W. Wolthers, Nieuwsblad van het Noorden 3 Apr 1926**: "Welte among many". It is an exhibition ad listing Welte-Mignon, Grotrian-Steinweg, Ibach, Gaveau and Stenberg, plus the New Edison phonograph.
- **Bender, Rotterdam, 28 Oct 1925** (NRC, Maasbode, Rotterdamsch Nieuwsblad): "Welte among many". It is a list of 16 trade-in instruments, with the Steinway Welte grand as item 1.
- **"W. R. Westhoff", Bataviaasch Nieuwsblad 4 Jan 1912**: misattributed in my first report. W. R. Westhoff is an adjacent cigar-shop ad. The Welte text belongs to a general Edmund Salzmann piano-shop ad (Grotrian, Ibach, Mannborg organs, gramophones …) in which the Welte-Mignon is one paragraph, so it is excluded as "Welte among many".
- **Guill. Stomps, Tageblatt Sept–Oct 1916**: "Welte among many" (Phonola, Pianola, Hupfeld, Steinway).
- **B. Schellenberg, L’Indépendance luxembourgeoise 3 Jan and 4 Feb 1928**: "Welte among many" (a brand list ending "Euphonator-Piano Welte — Mignon").
- **B. Schellenberg, "Pianos und Flügel", Obermosel-Zeitung 30 Nov–28 Dec 1929 and Tageblatt 12–28 Dec 1929**: "Welte among many". Welte-Mignon is a single line under thirteen piano brands, and the ad is about the shop's sole agencies.
- **Maison Paul Schanen, Luxemburger Zeitung 15 Aug and 2 Dec 1928**: "Welte among many" (a general piano and harmonium shop ad, "Pianos Gunther avec appareil électrique Welte-Mignon").
- **Gaveau, Le Journal 5 Nov 1928 and L’Intransigeant 11 Nov and 9 Dec 1928**: paid notice set as editorial text ("échos" listing weekly auditions; the Gaveau-Welte is one of several).
- **E. Moullé, Le Matin 27 Dec 1912 ("Les pianos Steinway")**: paid notice set as editorial text (advertorial). It is also mainly about Steinway.
- **S. Wolf, Der Republikaner (Mulhouse) 24 Dec 1930**: "Welte among many" (an occasions list: a Gaveau grand, an adaptable Welte-Mignon unit, Guarnerius and Steiner violins, a bow).
- **Mulhouse 1908 (J. Grunenwald, Express), 1913 (Neue Mülhauser Zeitung) and 1914 (S. Wolf shop opening, Oberelsässische Landes-Zeitung, Neue Mülhauser Zeitung, Express)**: outside scope (Alsace was then part of Germany), and in all of them the Welte-Mignon is one item among many.
- **Belgium**: nothing qualifies. Le Bruxellois 8–11 Mar 1915 is a private classified. Le Matin (Antwerp) 24 Dec 1912 is an editorial piece. The post-1918 BelgicaPress hits cannot be viewed online.
- **Previously excluded and not re-opened**: private classifieds (Algemeen Handelsblad, NRC, Haagsche Courant, Rotterdamsch Nieuwsblad, Le Figaro, Le Petit Marseillais, L’Intransigeant, Excelsior and others); auction notices; radio listings (the Belgian and French 1926 "piano Gunther Welte Mignon" programmes; the Luxembourg 1930 radio column); concert reports and news items (Luxembourg Dec 1924 – Jan 1925; Comoedia and Le Figaro 1927–28); café or hotel notices (Wester Paviljoen, Rotterdam, 1928, which only credit C. A. Muus for supplying the instrument); De Grondwet (Holland, Michigan, USA, 1927), which is outside the region.
