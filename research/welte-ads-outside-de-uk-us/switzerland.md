# Switzerland (CH): Welte-Mignon advertisements 1905–1930, transcriptions

Source of all scans: *Le Temps* archives (Gazette de Lausanne = GdL, Journal de Genève = JdG), IIIF server dedieletemps.cybor.ch, native-resolution crops of the ad zones.

### CH-01 — WELTE - MIGNON reproduit exactement le jeu des virtuoses
- **Periodical:** Gazette de Lausanne, Lausanne, 19 December 1909, p. 4
- **Advertiser:** Dingler & Cie, Magasin de pianos, Galeries du Commerce, Lausanne ("représentants")
- **Language:** French
- **Transcribed from:** https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/GDL_1909_12_19/4/article/439259
- **Further printings:**
  - GdL 1 Jan 1910, p. 4: https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/GDL_1910_01_01/4/article/1218210 (same text, "Schœrwenka").
  - GdL 3 Jan 1910, p. 4: https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/GDL_1910_01_03/4/article/1218451 (same text, "Schœrwenka").
  - GdL 6 Jan 1910, p. 4: https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/GDL_1910_01_06/4/article/1219061. Corrected line: "Scharwenka, etc."
  - GdL 7 Jan 1910, p. 4: https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/GDL_1910_01_07/4/article/1219567. Corrected line: "Scharwenka, etc."; the scan is creased across lines 1–3.
- **Notes:** The Welte-Mignon is the sole subject. Apart from the corrected name in the two January printings, I saw no other difference, so I treat those printings as a corrected state of the same design. "Paderewsky" is a common contemporary French spelling and is not marked.

```text
WELTE - MIGNON

reproduit exactement le jeu des virtuoses comme
Paderewsky
Busoni
Aug. d’Albert
Schœrwenka [sic], etc.

Auditions gratuites chez les représentants :

DINGLER & CIE
Magasin de pianos
Galeries du Commerce, Lausanne
```
Typography: Ruled box, all lines centred. "WELTE - MIGNON" is in very large condensed bold capitals, with spaces around the hyphen. Line 2 is in small condensed bold. The four pianist names are in a wide bold slab-serif (Clarendon-like) type, set as an indented left-aligned list. "Auditions gratuites chez les représentants :" is in large condensed bold. "DINGLER & CIE" is in large wide bold slab capitals, with "IE" as raised small capitals. "Magasin de pianos" is in bold slab. "Galeries du Commerce, Lausanne" is in large bold grotesque, across the full width.

### CH-02 — WELTE-MIGNON reproduit exactement le jeu des artistes
- **Periodical:** Gazette de Lausanne, Lausanne, 16 January 1910, p. 4
- **Advertiser:** Dingler & Cie, Galeries du Commerce, Lausanne
- **Language:** French
- **Transcribed from:** https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/GDL_1910_01_16/4/article/1221271. The archive's ad zone also contains the De Bay & Co perfume ad above it, which is not part of this ad.
- **Further printings:** none found.
- **Notes:**
  - The Welte-Mignon is the lead subject: the top headline, in the largest type, with the free demonstrations.
  - "PIANOS NEUFS" does not dominate, but it is a second offer of considerable weight. Its block is replaced by a gap, so this ad could also be read as "two products".
  - "S'adresser au magasin" refers to the demonstrations and is kept.

```text
WELTE-MIGNON
reproduit exactement le jeu des artistes

Auditions Gratuites
tous les mardis et vendredis à 4 heures
S’adresser au magasin

[gap: new pianos from fr. 650.—; tuning, repairs, hire]

Dingler & Cie

GALERIES DU COMMERCE
LAUSANNE
```
Typography: Full-column ad, centred. "WELTE-MIGNON" is in very large condensed bold capitals across the full width. Line 2 is in large condensed type. "Auditions Gratuites" is in bold extended type. "tous les mardis …" is in condensed bold. "S'adresser au magasin" is in letter-spaced bold. "Dingler & Cie" is in very large bold condensed slab, with a small raised "ie". "GALERIES DU COMMERCE" is in large condensed capitals. "LAUSANNE" is in an outlined (shadow) display face.

### CH-03 — Vente d'occasion. Nouveau "WELTE-MIGNON" (Vorsetzer)
- **Periodical:** Gazette de Lausanne, Lausanne, 13 February 1913, p. 6
- **Advertiser:** R. Reutemann, Fabrique de pianos, Freiestr. 58, Zurich V
- **Language:** French
- **Transcribed from:** https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/GDL_1913_02_13/6/article/1458458. The "Kränzchen" ad above shares the same ad zone in the archive but is not part of this ad.
- **Further printings:** none found.
- **Notes:**
  - This is a dealer's sale of a single new Welte-Mignon Vorsetzer, plus Mignon rolls (accessories to it), because Reutemann's Welte representation was ending.
  - "de la representation" is set very tight ("dela representation"). No accent is visible on the first e at high zoom.
  - "1793" is the ad's serial number.

```text
Vente d’occasion
Nouveau
“WELTE-MIGNON”
(Vorsetzer)
vendu pour cause de ces-
sation de la representation [sic]
avec 25 0/0 de rabais. Rou-
leaux de notes « Mignon »
25 0/0 de rabais.          1793

R. REUTEMANN
Fabrique de pianos
Freiestr. 58
ZURICH V
```
Typography: Framed, with heavy rules top and bottom and dotted side rules. Lines 1–4 and the address block are centred, and the body is justified. "Vente d'occasion" is in large condensed bold. "WELTE-MIGNON" is in large condensed bold capitals; its opening quotation mark is set low, at the bottom left of the word. "R. REUTEMANN" is in large bold slab capitals across the full width. "Freiestr. 58" is in italic. "ZURICH V" is in small bold capitals.

### CH-04 — A vendre d'occasion 1 piano à queue avec appareil WELTE-MIGNON
- **Periodical:** Journal de Genève, Geneva, 14 November 1925, p. 6
- **Advertiser:** not named. The only address is "PASSAGE DU TERRAILLET", the shop address of R. Kneifel-Béroud, Geneva (see CH-07).
- **Language:** French
- **Transcribed from:** https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1925_11_14/6/article/6143887
- **Further printings:** none found.
- **Notes:** The sole subject is a single (used) grand with Welte-Mignon action, so the ad qualifies on content. However, the advertiser is identified only by the address. Treat it as "Kneifel-Béroud (inferred)", or drop it if a printed name is required.

```text
A vendre d’occasion
1 piano à queue
avec appareil
WELTE-MIGNON

Prix très avantageux.          PASSAGE DU TERRAILLET
```
Typography: Ruled box, centred. "A vendre d'occasion" is in small bold. "1 piano à queue" is in very large heavy grotesque across the full width. "avec appareil" is in small roman. "WELTE-MIGNON" is in large heavy grotesque capitals. In the last line, "Prix très avantageux." (bold, left) and "PASSAGE DU TERRAILLET" (bold capitals, right) share one line.

### CH-05 — A vendre d'occasion … 1 piano à queue Welte-Mignon
- **Periodical:** Journal de Genève, Geneva, 27 March 1926, p. 7
- **Advertiser:** "magasin de pianos, Passage du Terraillet", the shop of R. Kneifel-Béroud, Geneva (inferred from the address; not named).
- **Language:** French
- **Transcribed from:** https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1926_03_27/7/article/6191131
- **Further printings:** JdG 31 Mar 1926, p. 7, at the head of the "A VENDRE" small-ads section: https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1926_03_31/7/article/6192639. Same text and setting.
- **Notes:**
  - This is a TWO PRODUCTS ad: a Steinway & Sons pianola and a Welte-Mignon grand, set at similar weight. The pianola part is replaced by a gap.
  - The advertiser is identified only by the address, as in CH-04. Drop it if a printed name is required.

```text
A vendre d’occasion

[gap: a Steinway & Sons pianola with 100 rolls]

1 piano à queue
Welte-Mignon
garanti à l’état de neuf.
Prix très avantageux.
S’adresser magasin de pianos,
Passage du Terraillet.
```
Typography: Single-column ad, centred. "Welte-Mignon" is in large condensed bold (the omitted pianola lines are in the same size). "1 piano à queue" is in bold. The remaining lines are in roman. In the last three lines, "Prix très avantageux." is centred and the two address lines are left-aligned.

### CH-06 — Salle du Conservatoire … RÉCITAL DE PIANO exécuté par le Steinway-Welte
- **Periodical:** Journal de Genève, Geneva, 6 November 1927 (Sunday), p. 3. It is the last entry of the boxed "Spectacles" advertising panel, below the Grand Théâtre, La Comédie and Cinéma Central ads.
- **Advertiser:** organised by "la maison Kneifel-Béroud, pianos, 20, Terraillet" (R. Kneifel-Béroud, Geneva)
- **Language:** French
- **Transcribed from:** https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1927_11_06/3/article/6411476
- **Further printings:** none found.
- **Notes:**
  - Kept as a dealer ad announcing a Welte demonstration recital. It is set as an ad: it is a display entry with its own display headings, inside a paid entertainment-ad panel, not running listing text. The last paragraph is in body type.
  - The print is worn. Several marks after names show no comma tail and may be commas or full stops; these are marked [,?]. The "0" of "20" and the accent of "étant" are worn.
  - "quelques maître" is singular as printed [sic].

```text
Salle du Conservatoire
Mardi 8 novembre[,?] à 20 h 30

RÉCITAL DE PIANO
exécuté par le
Steinway-Welte

reproduction intégrale et scrupuleusement fidèle
du jeu de quelques maître [sic] du clavier, dont : Eugène
d’Albert, Stavenhagen[,?] Debussy[,?] etc.
Au programme: Bach[,?] Mozart, Beethoven, Schu-
bert[,?] Chopin[,?] Liszt[,?] Debussy[,?] Stravinsky, etc.
Ce concert [é?]tant donné à bureaux fermés, prière
de s’adresser à la maison Kneifel-Béroud, pianos[,?]
2[0?], Terraillet, qui enverra des invitations à toutes
personnes qui en feront la demande.
```
Typography: Centred heading block. "Salle du Conservatoire" is in bold extended display type. "RÉCITAL DE PIANO" is in very large heavy extended capitals, letter-spaced across the full width. "exécuté par le" is in roman. "Steinway-Welte" is in a large letter-spaced light grotesque. The paragraph that follows is in justified roman body type, with "d'Albert … etc." centred. A heavy rule closes the panel.

### CH-07 — Le grand succès du Steinway-Welte
- **Periodical:** Journal de Genève, Geneva, 10 December 1927, p. 3
- **Advertiser:** Kneifel-Béroud, Magasin de Pianos, Passage du Terraillet (Geneva)
- **Language:** French
- **Transcribed from:** https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1927_12_10/3/article/6421238
- **Further printings:**
  - JdG 17 Dec 1927, p. 4: https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1927_12_17/4/article/6423230
  - JdG 24 Dec 1927, p. 5: https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1927_12_24/5/article/6425474
  - JdG 31 Dec 1927, p. 3: https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1927_12_31/3/article/6427151
  - All have the same text and setting.
- **Notes:**
  - It follows up the recital (CH-06).
  - "de jeu" (for "du jeu") is printed with a very narrow word space ("dejeu") and is marked [sic].
  - The circumflex of "maîtres" is worn in this printing but clearly legible in the 31 Dec printing.

```text
Le grand succès du
Steinway-Welte

au dernier concert au Conservatoire a
prouvé son éclatante supériorité dans la
reproduction de jeu [sic] des maîtres du clavier
Voir les différents modèles chez

KNEIFEL-BÉROUD
Magasin de Pianos, Passage du Terraillet
```
Typography: Ruled ad. "Steinway-Welte" is in very large letter-spaced grotesque across the full width. The body is in roman, and "KNEIFEL-BÉROUD" is in letter-spaced grotesque capitals, centred. Black block ornaments sit on the left: a small pair beside line 1 and a tall pair beside the body.

### CH-08 — MM. les Cafetiers, Restaurateurs et Hôteliers … ATTENTION … EXPOSITION PERMANENTE
- **Periodical:** Journal de Genève, Geneva, 18 December 1930, p. 7
- **Advertiser:** Charrière & Co, Bulle, "première maison suisse spécialisée". The exhibition was held at 9, rue des Alpes, Geneva.
- **Language:** French
- **Transcribed from:** https://www.letempsarchives.ch/page/JDG_1930_12_18/7/article/5601400
- **Further printings:** none found up to 1930 (later printings in January 1931 would be outside the range).
- **Notes:**
  - Kept as TWO PRODUCTS: the amplifier sets "CREATONAL" and the piano-phonos "WELTE-MIGNON combinés SUPER-CREATONAL" are the only products named, both at the same weight. The amplifier part is replaced by a gap.
  - This is borderline. The main subject is the exhibition as a whole, and even the Welte product is sold as a Creatonal combination, so exclude it if the Welte-Mignon must dominate.
  - In this typeface the C is nearly closed and looks like O ("AUOUN", "OREATONAL"); it is transcribed as C.
  - The second digit of the ad code is uncertain.

```text
MM. les Cafetiers, Restaurateurs et Hôteliers du Canton de Genève

ATTENTION

Du 15 décembre au 31 janvier
la Maison CHARRIÈRE & Co, de Bulle
tiendra une
EXPOSITION PERMANENTE

des dernières merveilles de la technique moderne du domaine des instruments de musique
pour établissements publics,
de sensationnelles nouveautés en [gap: amplifier sets « CREATONAL »] et pianos-phonos
« WELTE-MIGNON combinés SUPER-CREATONAL »
dans les magasins anciennement « Willys-Knight » de l’American Motors Trading Co,

9, rue des Alpes - GENÈVE

ENTRÉE LIBRE ET SANS AUCUN ENGAGEMENT

[bottom left] MD1[0?]40B
[bottom right] CHARRIÈRE & Co, Bulle
première maison suisse spécialisée
```
Typography:
- Heavy frame; a very large black exclamation mark stands at each side of the headline block.
- Line 1 is in tall condensed type across the full width. "ATTENTION" is in very large heavy slab capitals. "Du 15 décembre au 31 janvier" is in bold grotesque. "la Maison CHARRIÈRE & Co, de Bulle" is in large grotesque, with a small "la" and a superscript "o" in "Co". "tiendra une" is letter-spaced. "EXPOSITION PERMANENTE" is in large bold grotesque capitals.
- The body is centred and in roman, with the product names in capitals.
- "9, rue des Alpes - GENÈVE" is in heavy grotesque. "ENTRÉE LIBRE …" is in letter-spaced grotesque capitals inside a ruled box.
- The signature is in bold grotesque, with "première maison …" in small bold.
