### Example: an omitted part

A part of an advertisement that concerns other products is not transcribed.
A `<gap>` takes its place, and its `<desc>` says briefly what is left out:

```xml
<gap reason="irrelevant">
    <desc resp="#np">
        The ad also includes texts for "Pathe",
        "Pianos and Player-Pianos" and
        "Victor Victrolas and Victor Records".
    </desc>
</gap>
```
