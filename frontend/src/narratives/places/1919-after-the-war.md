After the war the ads come back first in the north and in Havana, where
one dealer's ad ran 16 times from November 1919. From 1921 the Freiburg
firm advertises under its own name in Berlin's *Sport im Bild*, and in
1923 even in three music and theatre magazines in Petrograd.
