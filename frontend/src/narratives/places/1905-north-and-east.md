The collection's first ad appeared in Munich in December 1905 and names
25 artists. Within three years, dealers advertised the instrument in
Poznań, Riga, Stockholm, Kristiania and St Petersburg. The large circle of
1908 is a single ad by Zimmermann of Riga and St Petersburg, printed 31
times in a theatre review.
