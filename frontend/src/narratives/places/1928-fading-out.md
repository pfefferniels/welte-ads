After 1927 the ads thin out: 13 ads in 1928, a single one in 1929, and the
last printing in the collection appeared in Sydney on 3 July 1930. Radio,
the electric gramophone and the economic crisis had nearly ended the firm,
and by 1932 Welte built only organs.
