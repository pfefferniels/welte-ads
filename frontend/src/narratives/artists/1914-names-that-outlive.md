The immortality of the artists is a recurring topic of the ads (see
[immortality](/topic/immortality/)). In and after the war it becomes real
for many of them: Pugno died in 1914, Leschetizky in 1915, Reger in 1916,
Carreño in 1917, Debussy in 1918, Saint-Saëns in 1921 and Nikisch in 1922.
The ads keep their names, as they kept Grieg's: he died in 1907 and is
named 21 times, 18 of them after his death.
