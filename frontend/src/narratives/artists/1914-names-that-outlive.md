While the war silences the ads, daggers spread through the cloud: Pugno
died in 1914, Leschetizky in 1915, Reger in 1916, Carreño in 1917 and
Debussy in 1918. The ads kept the names, for a roll plays on: Grieg, who
died in 1907, is named 21 times, 18 of them after his death (see the topic
[immortality](/topic/immortality/)).
