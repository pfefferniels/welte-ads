Topics, five ways

How the Topics page of the Welte-Mignon Advertisements Collection would look if its topics came from different methods. Every view uses the edition's own circle layout and is built from pilot runs on the 195 advertisements. Click a circle to read what it contains. Circle sizes compare within one view only. “Your topics” counts marked passages, as the site does; the other views count advertisements.

    Clusters: 1,677 sentences and picture descriptions embedded with paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2 and grouped with BERTopic (UMAP + HDBSCAN, seed 0, minimum cluster size 10). Seeded candidates: unmarked sentences most similar to your marked passages, read and judged by hand, plus sentences repeated verbatim elsewhere with a topic. LLM view: one blind TopicGPT-style reading of all ads by Claude, coded per ad, compared with your taxonomy afterwards.

    All numbers come from single exploratory runs, not settled results. The research notes and scripts are in the repository under research_notes/. Ad links open the edition at anz.welte225.org.
